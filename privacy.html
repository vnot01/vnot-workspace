2. Faceless AI Studio & Third-Party API Services (YouTube, Meta, Threads)

Our application Faceless AI Studio (accessible at aistudio.vnot.my.id) utilizes third-party API Services including YouTube, Meta (Facebook & Instagram), and Threads to provide features such as automated video uploads and metadata management.

By using Faceless AI Studio and connecting your accounts, you are agreeing to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, Google Privacy Policy, Meta Terms of Service, Meta Privacy Policy, and the Threads Terms of Use.

Data Usage & Revocation: Faceless AI Studio only accesses and uses your third-party data (such as authentication tokens and basic profile info) for the explicit purpose of managing your channel's content as initiated by you within the app. We do not share this data with unauthorized external parties. You can revoke Faceless AI Studio's access to your Google data at any time via the Google Security Settings page, and for Meta/Threads via your Facebook or Instagram account settings under "Apps and Websites".

3. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect via the Applications includes:

  • Personal Data: Personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, and demographic information that you voluntarily give to us when you register for the Applications or when you choose to participate in various activities related to the Applications (such as linking a Google Account for OAuth authentication).
  • Derivative Data: Information our servers automatically collect when you access the Applications, such as your IP address, your browser type, your operating system, your access times, and the pages you have viewed directly before and after accessing the Applications.
  • OAuth Data: If you choose to log in using Google, Facebook, or other third-party providers (including YouTube API access for Faceless AI Studio), we receive information from those providers strictly necessary to authenticate you and perform requested actions. We do not request or store passwords for these third-party accounts.

Having accurate information about you permits us to provide you with a smooth, efficient, and customized experience. Specifically, we may use information collected about you via the Applications to:

  • Create and manage your account.

5. Disclosure of Your Information

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties except as necessary to provide the services you have requested, comply with the law, or protect our rights.

6. Security of Your Information

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures (including Zero-Trust architectures in TrustlessSign and decentralized storage in VnotDrive) to help protect your personal information. While we have taken reasonable steps to secure the personal information you provide to us, please be aware that despite our efforts, no security measures are perfect or impenetrable.

7. User Data Deletion Instructions

If you have connected your Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Threads) or Google accounts to Faceless AI Studio and wish to delete your data, you can do so at any time. We provide two ways to request the deletion of your user data:

  • Directly within the App: Log in to Faceless AI Studio, navigate to the "Social Scheduler" > "Connected Accounts" section, and click the "Unlink" button next to your connected accounts. This will immediately remove your authentication tokens and associated profile data from our servers.
  • Via Email Request: You can send an email requesting data deletion to our support team. Please contact us via our main portal and provide your account details. We will process your data deletion request within 7 business days.

Alternatively, you can remove our app's access directly from your Facebook account settings by going to Settings & Privacy > Settings > Apps and Websites, selecting our app, and clicking "Remove".
